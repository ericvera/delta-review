Notes
  • What triggers it: placing the cursor or a selection in either side of a file's text in the combined diff, by mouse or keyboard. The left (base) side selects the row too. Moving into another file's text moves the row selection to that file.
  • Header clicks do not select: clicking only a file's header (the yellow-dashed validate.ts header above: its name, chevron or ) leaves the pane's selection where it was. VS Code does not tell extensions about header clicks. Click into the file's text instead.
  • No focus change: the pane does not take keyboard focus. Typing still goes to the diff, and the row shows the grey inactive selection, not the blue focused one. If the Delta Review view is collapsed or hidden, nothing is revealed or opened.
  • Only in the combined diff: clicks in ordinary editors and single-file diffs do not move the pane's selection.
  • After Mark Reviewed: the row behaves as it does today: it updates to reviewed and the cluster count goes to 3/6. The combined diff tab is not changed by marking one file.
